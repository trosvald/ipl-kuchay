|----------|-------------|
| resident browser → invoice reads | Residents may attempt to access invoice data outside their kavling/history scope. |
| billing data → visual summary logic | Incorrect client-side aggregation can misstate what the resident owes. |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-02-08 | I | resident invoice list/detail queries | mitigate | Keep reads scoped by existing RLS/history helpers and avoid bypass queries outside resident-visible relations. |
| T-02-09 | T | arrears and outstanding calculations | mitigate | Derive displayed totals from fetched invoice fields and item rows only; centralize labels in `lib/format.ts`. |
| T-02-10 | R | resident interpretation of billing state | accept | Operational confusion risk is reduced through explicit Indonesian headings, focal-point arrears summary, and consistent status copy. |
</threat_model>
